The grammar that turns passive responses into powerful communication
⏱ 20–25 minutes
Part 1: Spot and Fix the Errors
⏱ 5–7 MINUTES
Before learning the structure, try to identify what's wrong in each sentence. Trust your instinct — you already know more than you think.

What's wrong?

"If we changing the supplier, we would improve quality."
What's wrong?
Part 2: The Structure
⏱ 5 MINUTES
IF + PAST SIMPLE → WOULD + BASE VERB
If I had more confidence, I would speak more during the meeting.
If I were in a meeting with an important client, I would prepare more.
If we could change our approach, we would improve our results.
Key patterns: If + had (If I had more time) · If + were (If I were in Germany) · If + changed (If we changed our approach)
Now go back to your error corrections above. Were you right? Fix any corrections if needed.

Part 2: Read and Compare
⏱ 8–10 MINUTES
🇧🇷 Brazil — Warmth & Connection
First impressions are built on personal connection. Brazilians value small talk before business. Eye contact shows confidence and honesty. Open body language signals interest. Being too formal or distant can seem unfriendly or arrogant.
🇩🇪 Germany — Precision & Professionalism
Punctuality and preparation are paramount. Germans appreciate clarity and dislike wasted time. Formal titles and proper etiquette matter. A firm handshake and professional appearance are expected. Being too casual can make people question your competence.
🇯🇵 Japan — Respect & Formality
Respect and humility are central. The exchange of business cards (meishi) is a ritual performed with both hands. Bowing shows respect. Japanese professionals value long-term relationships. Being too loud, assertive, or casual can be seen as disrespectful.
